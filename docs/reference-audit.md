# Product reference audit: rumahadat.id / Rumah Bolon

Checked on 24 October 2026 at `https://rumahadat.id/rumah-bolon`.

## Reference features

- A real-time 3D object remains central while information and controls sit in separate side regions.
- The page states that its model is an ideal-type reconstruction and explains estimation limits.
- Buildings/components are listed semantically and can be focused or hidden.
- Controls are grouped by purpose: view, roofs, display, section cut, materials, and lighting.
- Camera presets, a guided walk, keyboard help, zoom, reset/frame, fullscreen, screenshots, and model download are available.
- The compound structure is searchable and grouped by functional category.

## Adopt

- Keep limitations visible beside the interpretive model.
- Keep 3D interaction and semantic information connected.
- Provide camera focus from architectural components and timeline events.
- Preserve keyboard-accessible navigation and explicit interaction help.
- Separate free exploration from architecture and history learning modes.

## Adapt

- Adapt component selection into restrained, evidence-linked architectural hotspots.
- Adapt the three-column information/object/control principle to the existing dark Indonesian museum identity.
- Adapt guided exploration into user-controlled modes and timeline focus; no autoplay.
- Adapt searchable object structure into collection metadata search and an atlas for the current verified records.

## Reject

- Do not copy branding, bright visual composition, typography, assets, wording, or layout measurements.
- Do not add section cuts, exploded roofs, editable materials, model downloads, or render-style switches because current geometry and product requirements do not justify them.
- Do not reproduce the dense technical-control surface; this project prioritizes heritage learning and provenance.

## Not applicable

- Roof lifting and roof opacity do not map to the current monument and clock-tower geometry.
- Section cuts would expose no documented internal structure in the current procedural models.
- X-ray, blueprint, hide/isolate, and editable roughness would currently change presentation without adding verified knowledge.
- 3D downloads are withheld because there is no tested export pipeline, explicit download policy, or packaged attribution/license record.
- Time-of-day simulation is withheld because it has no calibrated geographic/date model in this phase.
- Guided walks and historical states are withheld until the available evidence supports complete, landmark-specific chapters or reconstructions.
